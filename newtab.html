Todoist tips

How your Todoist is read

  • A section is a project here. Keep your bigger projects (say Garden, Bike and Novel) as sections inside one project called Big Projects. The free plan allows 5 projects, so sections are how you get more.
  • The top task is the next step. Drag the real next step to the top of each section. That is what nudges and the new tab show.
  • Quiet means nothing completed. Adding or editing tasks does not count as progress, finishing one does.
  • Projects or sections named Fun, Movies, Games, Books or Watch never get nudges. They show up as evening suggestions instead.

Labels

@15m @30m @1h @2h @90mTime estimate. Only labelled tasks appear under "I have 30m".
@lowLow-energy task, offered as a quick win.
@softThe due date is a plan, not a deadline. No deadline reminders.

Deadlines

  • Any one-off due date counts as a deadline. Recurring tasks never do.
  • Add a time if it matters: Friday 14:00.
  • You get a reminder 7 days before, 3 days, 1 day, on the day, then daily while overdue (weekly after two weeks). Moving the date restarts the countdown.
  • Todoist Pro has a separate deadline field, typed in curly brackets: {next friday}. If set, it wins over the due date.
  • Deadline reminders ignore "Snooze 2h". They pause only during a break you started.

Adding tasks here

  • + Add task or the N key opens the form. Pick the project, time, due date and energy with buttons; it sets the right Todoist labels for you.
  • + New project creates a section (for example inside Big Projects). It gets its own row and staleness tracking straight away.
  • Make it the next step puts the task at the top of its project. Otherwise it joins the end of the queue.
  • Steps, one per line, become subtasks. Notes become the task description.
  • A project row that says Write the next step opens the form for that project.

Focus timer

  • The ▶ next to any task starts a focus timer. Nudges pause while it runs.
  • Done completes the task and counts the time. Ticking the task off anywhere does the same.
  • Stop asks what you got done and saves it as a completed subtask, so the time counts and the project shows progress. Or discard the time.
  • Only time that ends in something completed counts. The chart in the left column shows the last 7 days of focus and breaks.

Your day

  • Top 3: pick up to three tasks each day. They sit above everything and the nudges focus on them. Stored only in Nudge, synced through Chrome. A day runs from 04:00 to 04:00.
  • Done today lists what you finished. Projects with something done several days running show a streak.
  • Two-minute tasks: label a task @2m (or pick 2m in the form). One pops up at most once an hour: just do it.
  • Shutdown at 00:00: a notification and a card with today's summary. Pick tomorrow's Top 3, then Close up; nudges stay quiet until 08:00.
  • Breaks: 3 hours a day. Once that's used, only 15-minute breaks are offered.
  • Weekly review on Sunday at 21:00 (or any time from the top bar): the week's numbers, empty the Inbox, check each project's next step, clear stuck steps, pick Monday's Top 3.

Habits that make this work

  • Weekly review. Add a recurring task: Weekly review every sunday @30m. Empty the Inbox, pick next steps, delete what no longer matters.
  • Write steps, not topics. "Rocket nozzle" is a topic. "Sketch nozzle profile on paper" is a step.
  • If a step sits for a week, it is too big or unclear. Split it or rewrite it. The stuck-step nudge tells you which.
  • Deleting a task you will never do is progress too.

Notifications

  • Open in Todoist jumps to the task. Snooze 2h pauses regular nudges.
  • At most one regular nudge per 2 hours of active PC use. Change it in Settings.

Reddit and Facebook

  • They open a pause screen with your next step and three hand-picked sites instead. The same picks sit at the bottom of every new tab.
  • Thumbs up makes a site (and its kind) show up more, up to 4× as often. Thumbs down makes it rare; twice hides it. Restore hidden ones in Settings.
  • Start a break to get through. They open freely until the break ends.
  • The Music button in the corner opens Spotify on a fresh playlist search. Surprise me rolls a genre you probably don't listen to.
  • Add or remove sites in Settings, under Pause list.
  • On the new tab the picks sit in the right column.
